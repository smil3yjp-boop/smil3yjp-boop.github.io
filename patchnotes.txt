v0.3.4 | Nov 5, 2026
- Lych heals half as much when it absorbs its bloons
- Bosses can no longer heal back past a skull they've already dropped (75%, 50%, 25%)
- Hero levels need even more XP from level 100 onward (x4 instead of x2)
- Runs now always take place on Overlord's Ring (the other maps are kept for later)
- Bloons now come out of four portals at the north, south, east and west of the arena instead of around you
- MOAB-class bloons and bosses come out of a portal in the middle of the arena
- After a boss, the Continue and End Run portals open on either side of the middle portal
- The spawn portals are shown on the minimap
- The mouse cursor is now a crosshair during runs (the normal cursor comes back on menus, cards and pause)
- New Crosshair settings: style (cross, dot, circle, circle + cross, T), colour (presets or any custom colour), size, thickness, center gap, opacity, outline and center dot, with a live preview
- Blimps and bosses now always make their hit sound when damaged, and groan as their hull breaks at 75%, 50% and 25% health
- Fixed the Skill Effects slider: it now dims your own and other players' skill effects (it wasn't applying before)
- Fixed Dartling Gunner models that only showed their base: Ray of Doom, Buckshot, Bloon Area Denial System and Bloon Exclusion Zone now show the monkey and gun
- Fixed the Sniper Paragon not holding its rifle
- Co-op: ending the run or quitting now only takes you out of the run; everyone else keeps playing (if you were running the world, the next player takes over)
- Co-op: the Continue portal now needs every player to be ready, with a ready counter above the portal
- Co-op: players who join mid-run now stay on the main menu and can press Spectate Run next to Patch Notes
- Co-op: the run gets easier again when a player leaves
- Co-op: fixed bosses only dying when the host landed the killing blow
- Co-op: bloons no longer vanish and pop back in for non-host players when they pop a layer
- New bloons now grow in when they spawn instead of appearing at full size
- Co-op: the world now updates 15 times a second for other players (was 10), for smoother bloons
- Co-op: every player now shows as a coloured blip on the minimap (blinking red while down)
- Co-op: party panel at the top left during a run with each player's Discord avatar, name and health
- Co-op leaderboard: best party runs by Team Damage, Level, Loops, Bosses and Time Survived, with every player's avatar
- Click a party on the Co-op leaderboard to see each player's own end-of-run card (MVP marked)
- Co-op runs now count on the leaderboard even when players leave through the End Run portal or Quit at different times
- Bloon spawn portals and the middle MOAB portal now look like the Continue portal, in purple with small cyan sparks
- The Skill Effects slider no longer fades enemy shots (the pink orbs always show at full strength)
- Captain Churchill nerf: Tank Shells deal 11 damage (was 14), fire every 1.35s (was 1.2s) with a smaller blast (2.1, was 2.4); his area bonus is +10% (was +15%); Armor Piercing Shells cooldown 45s (was 35s)
- Monkey Sub nerf: torpedoes deal 8 damage (was 9) and the torpedo itself now only does about a third of that on impact before it explodes (the blast is unchanged)
- New boss theme: every boss now plays the same new track
- Music volume now starts at 10% (only changes it if you haven't set your own music volume)
